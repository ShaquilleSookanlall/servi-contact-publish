DaBill Serv

DaBill Serv is a monthly subscription service that gives you access to exclusive discount vouchers for popular restaurants and movie tickets. By signing up via a secure debit order, you receive discounted dining and entertainment deals every month.

  • 50% Off Restaurant Meals
  • Buy-One-Get-One-Free Movie Tickets
  • Affordable Monthly Savings

Connect Serv

Connect Serv is a connectivity service that uses eSIM technology to provide fast, flexible, and secure internet and voice solutions without needing a physical SIM card. Stay connected with data, calls, and support through a digital, built-in SIM.

  • High-Speed Internet Access
  • Flexible & Convenient Usage
  • 24/7 Support & Security

Servi Excess Cover

Servi Excess Cover is an affordable monthly protection plan that provides cash payouts in the event of unexpected emergencies such as hospital admissions, car accidents, home incidents, and funeral-related costs. It helps ease the financial burden when unforeseen situations arise.

  • Affordable Cover
  • Cash Paid Directly to You
  • Wide Emergency Coverage

Servi Loyalty Rewards

Servi Loyalty Rewards is a membership program that gives you access to everyday savings and financial support benefits, including discounted shopping vouchers, airtime deals, cinema discounts, and potential loan assistance.

  • Discounted Shopping & Airtime
  • Entertainment Savings
  • Financial Assistance Access

Servi Smart Shield

Servi Smart Shield is a low-cost monthly protection plan that covers your phone screen against accidental cracks or damage, offering quick and easy repairs without complicated paperwork.

  • Affordable Protection
  • Fast & Easy Claims
  • Multiple Devices Covered

Servi Death Cover

Servi Death Benefit Policy provides essential funeral cover designed to secure a client’s legacy and support their loved ones in times of need. Coverage for accidental death is immediate upon the first premium, while natural death is covered after a 3-month waiting period. Multiple packages available.

  • Immediate Accidental Death Cover
  • Tailored Packages
  • Family Protection
